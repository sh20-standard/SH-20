**circuit-1.** Nothing in the relation ties input 0 to input 1. A witness that spends the same note,
at the same position, as both inputs satisfies every gate: its value is counted twice in the
conservation, and its two nullifiers are equal. What refuses it is §5, "nullifiers non-zero,
distinct, never seen", which both indexers enforce (`indexer/src/indexer.js` l. 277,
`indexer-rs/src/state.rs` l. 844) and the vectors test (`nfTwice` in `indexer/vectors/v0.3.json`).
An implementation that checked only "never seen" would let anyone double a note's value once per
spend. Today this is as designed and covered, so it is a Note; it is told here because it is the one
place where the supply rests on a rule outside the proof. Fix, in order: one sentence in §5 saying
that the relation does not refuse a doubled input and that this rule does (no byte changes; added
with this report); at the next change of the keys, `nf0 − nf1 ≠ 0` in the circuit as well (one gate and one inverse), which
needs the maintainer (`CLAUDE.md`, rule 4).

*Fixed on 2026-11-23, by the maintainer's decision, in the pull request with `circuit-1` in its title: `nf0 ≠ nf1` is in
the relation (`spend.rs`, "nullifiers distinct": a subtraction and a product with an inverse, two gates), with new keys and
the digest `64eaf3fe…513b31f4`. The test is now `audit_one_note_as_both_inputs_is_refused`: that witness breaks this
gate and no other. §5 of `SPEC-WIRE.md` keeps the indexers' rule, checked before the proof. The lines this report cites
are those of its commit.*

**circuit-2.** With `vPub = 0` the relation leaves `tPub` free, and with two dummy inputs it leaves
`t` free: such a spend can only create notes of value 0 (the test checks that one unit more is
refused), but under any asset id, `t = 0` (reserved for BTC) and tickers never deployed included. No
supply moves, and the indexers fix `(vPub, tPub)` by envelope type (`docs/AUDIT.md` §2, R6). A wallet
that shows the notes it scans should not show a note of value 0, and never as BTC.

## 4. Holds

1. **One `t`.** `head = Poseidon(TAG_LEAF, t)` is computed once (`spend.rs` l. 49–50) and enters the
   five leaves through `note_leaf`. Cases "private ticker changed", "outputs under another ticker".
2. **`nk < l`.** `scalar_bits` (`gadgets.rs` l. 136) decomposes `nk` into 251 bits and range-checks
   `l − 1 − nk` to 251 bits. `l < 2^251` (computed), so `nk ≤ l − 1` passes; for `l ≤ nk < 2^251` the
   difference wraps to at least `r − 2^251 > 2^251` (computed) and fails. So `nk` is the unique
   scalar of `NK`, and `NK = nk·B8` is in the prime subgroup. Case "nullifier key shifted by the group order".
3. **Leaf.** `note_leaf` (`spend.rs` l. 29–33) is the formula of §4; the tag matches the indexer
   (`tests/spend.rs`, `leaf_tag_matches_the_indexer`), and the real proofs of
   `indexer/vectors/plonk-v0.3.json` verify against leaves the indexer computed.
4. **Membership.** `to_bits(index, 32)` (`gadgets.rs` l. 102) makes the bits boolean and their sum
   equal to `index` in the field, so `index < 2^32` with one decomposition; the new test
   `audit_a_position_shifted_by_2_32_is_refused` checks that `index + 2^32`, which would give the note
   a second nullifier, is refused. `merkle_root` (l. 285) puts `(cur, sib)` or `(sib, cur)` by the bit,
   level 0 first. The dummy gate `diff − diff·[v = 0] = 0` (`spend.rs` l. 77) has its selectors in the
   order `ql qr qo qm qc` of `cs.rs` l. 30–34; `is_zero` (`cs.rs` l. 192) gives 0 whenever `v ≠ 0`
   (from `v·out = 0`), whatever inverse the prover offers. Cases "wrong root", "wrong sibling",
   "wrong position", "note with value passed off as a dummy".
5. **Nullifier.** `Poseidon(nk, leaf, index)` (`spend.rs` l. 80) uses the variable `index` whose bits
   walk the path. With properties 2 and 4, a note has one nullifier. Case "second nullifier for the
   same note (other position)".
6. **Signature.** `eddsa_verify` (`gadgets.rs` l. 261): both points on the curve; `S < l` by
   `scalar_bits`; `h` by `canonical_bits_254` (l. 145), whose two checks (`hi ≤ r_hi`, and `lo < r_lo`
   when `hi = r_hi`) leave one decomposition, the integer below `r` (`r_hi < 2^126`, computed); `4·Ak`
   with a non-zero x refuses the eight points of small order; `8·Ak` removes any small-order part
   before `h·8·Ak`. `R8` need not be checked to be in the subgroup: `R8 = S·B8 − h·8·Ak` is a
   difference of two subgroup points. An `Ak` outside the subgroup (`docs/AUDIT.md` §6.4) still needs
   the discrete logarithm of its subgroup part to sign, and `npk` binds `Ak` exactly, so it neither
   forges nor gives a note a second key. The additions are complete: `a = 168700` is a square and
   `d = 168696` is not, modulo `r` (computed by Euler's criterion). Cases "signature S shifted by the
   group order", "signature R off the curve", "spend key off the curve", and the small-order keys of
   `tests/gadgets.rs`.
7. **Amounts.** `range(·, 64)` on the five amounts and `vPub` (`spend.rs` l. 56, 95, 105); both sides
   of the conservation are below `2^66 < r`, so equality in the field is equality of integers. Cases
   "negative output balancing a larger one", "output of 2^64", "output of p − 1", "largest amounts".
8. **Public value.** `vPub·(t − tPub) = 0` (`spend.rs` l. 107–108). Cases "burn under another public
   ticker", "value taken out without a public amount".
9. **Public inputs.** Declared first and in the order of §4 (`spend.rs` l. 41–46); each enters a gate:
   `root` the membership, `nf` the nullifiers, the leaves the outputs, `bodyHash` the signature, `vPub`
   the range, the ticker rule and the conservation, `tPub` the ticker rule.
10. **Poseidon.** Partial rounds 57, 56, 60, 60 for widths 3 to 6 (`poseidon.rs` l. 16–21), as
    circomlib; the S-box `(x + rc)^5` in three gates (`gadgets.rs` l. 53–55) checks out algebraically;
    outputs against circomlibjs for every width (`tests/vectors.rs`).
11. **The unconstrained variable.** Variable 0 fills unused wire slots and is never constrained
    (`cs.rs` l. 65); every gate constructor of `cs.rs` gives it a zero coefficient, and no product
    `qm·a·b` has it as a factor with a non-zero `qm`. The only other free witnesses are the inverse of
    `is_zero` when its input is 0 and a dummy's siblings, which reach no check.
